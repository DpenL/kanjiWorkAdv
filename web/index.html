漢字コモンズ / Kanji Commons

A meeting place for people building and studying kanji learning games

This page is the front door — read the 60-second orientation below before you enter the 2D space, and the space will feel easy instead of confusing.

What to expect

1 · It looks like a retro game — it isn't a test You move a little character around a map. Walking near people starts a voice/video bubble automatically; walking away ends it. That's the whole trick.
2 · Your browser will ask for camera & microphone Click Allow when asked. You can mute yourself anytime with the buttons at the bottom of the screen.
3 · Moving around Use the arrow keys or W A S D. When a message says “Press SPACE”, press the space bar to open a kiosk. Nothing is timed and nothing can be broken.

Enter the space

You'll arrive in the genkan (entryway): a small room where you pick a language and try a voice bubble. Step through the red torii when ready. A 🔔 Help button is always in your toolbar.

New to games? / ゲームが初めての方へ

This space is deliberately gentle: no timers, no fail states, no locked doors. If you ever feel lost, press 🔔 Help or just type in the chat — someone will come to you. 日本語でもどうぞ。

Accessibility

Keyboard-only navigation works throughout. Every kiosk's content also exists as normal web pages (see the Library), so nothing essential is locked inside the game world.
