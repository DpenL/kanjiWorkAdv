カラオケ · Karaoke corner

After-hours zone — the purple corner of the common room

How it works

1 · The corner is its own voice roomEveryone standing in the purple zone shares one audio bubble (Jitsi), separate from the rest of the commons.
2 · One person is the DJThe DJ plays a track and shares it via the bubble's screen-share → share tab audio option so everyone hears the same thing.
3 · Everyone else singsNo skill requirements. This corner exists so a workshop can end warm instead of just ending.

Track sources

Use official artist/label or licensed karaoke channels for backing tracks — this keeps the corner unproblematic for a public research community. Paste your community playlist link here once you have one.

Beyond MVP

Ideas queued in docs/DESIGN.md: a song-request board, a “nomikai” izakaya table next door for talking between songs, and seasonal decorations (sakura in spring, 紅葉 in autumn).
